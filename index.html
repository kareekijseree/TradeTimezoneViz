        My trading window

        Session hours follow common FX conventions (Sydney 07:00–16:00, Tokyo 09:00–18:00, London 08:00–17:00 and New York 08:00–17:00, each in local time, with daylight saving applied automatically). The spot FX week runs from Sunday 17:00 to Friday 17:00 New York time. Bank holidays and central-bank meeting calendars are not tracked here, so check an economic calendar before trading news. The activity curve is a rough estimate based on which centres are open, not live volume data. This is an educational aid, not trading advice.
